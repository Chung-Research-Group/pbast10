How to make a reservation

Download the PBAST10 reservation form in PDF or Word format, complete your guest and stay details, and email your reservation request directly to the hotel contacts below. Leave the credit-card guarantee section blank in emailed copies and contact the hotel to arrange secure submission of payment details. The hotel will confirm availability and your booking directly.
