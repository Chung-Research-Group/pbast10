Accommodation

Somerset Palace Seoul

Somerset Palace Seoul is the official conference hotel for PBAST10. Participants can request the special conference rates using the reservation form below. All reservations are handled directly by the hotel.

Reservation deadline: April 30, 2027. Conference rates apply to stays from May 30 to June 4, 2027, subject to availability and hotel confirmation.

PBAST10 Conference Hotel

Somerset Palace Seoul

7, Yulgok-ro 2-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul 03143, Republic of Korea Route ↗

Located in central Seoul, Somerset Palace Seoul offers serviced apartments with kitchen facilities. Each apartment includes home entertainment equipment, a washer, and a tumble dryer.

The property provides a rooftop swimming pool, fitness centre, billiards room, rooftop hot tub, garden, restaurants, and a café. It is within walking distance of Anguk Station, Insa-dong, Jongno, and Gwanghwamun Station.
