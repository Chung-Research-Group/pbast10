Accommodation

Where to stay in Seoul

Somerset Palace Seoul is the main hotel for PBAST10. Six additional hotels are recommended for participants who prefer to stay near Sinchon or Hongdae.

Hotel rates and availability may change. Please confirm current details directly with the hotel or booking provider.

PBAST10 Main Hotel

Somerset Palace Seoul

7, Yulgok-ro 2-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul 03143, Republic of Korea

Located in central Seoul, Somerset Palace Seoul offers serviced apartments with kitchen facilities. Each apartment includes home entertainment equipment, a washer, and a tumble dryer.

The property provides a rooftop swimming pool, fitness centre, billiards room, rooftop hot tub, garden, restaurants, and a café. It is within walking distance of Anguk Station, Insa-dong, Jongno, and Gwanghwamun Station.

Serviced apartmentsKitchen and in-room laundry facilities
Central Seoul locationNear Anguk, Insa-dong, Jongno, and Gwanghwamun
