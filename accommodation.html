Accommodation

Stay at Somerset Palace Seoul

Somerset Palace Seoul is the main hotel for PBAST10. Participants are invited to book at the special conference rates using the reservation form below. All reservations are handled directly by the hotel.

PBAST10 Main Hotel

Somerset Palace Seoul

7, Yulgok-ro 2-gil, Jongno-gu, Seoul 03143, Republic of Korea Route ↗

Located in central Seoul, Somerset Palace Seoul offers serviced apartments with kitchen and in-room laundry facilities. It is within walking distance of Anguk Station, Insa-dong, Jongno, and Gwanghwamun Station.

The conference reservation form includes room options, breakfast arrangements, payment and cancellation terms, and an optional airport taxi service request.
